The forecast is updated as new data arrives and as its methods are checked. Each change that
moves a published figure will be listed here, newest first, with what moved and why.

The first version was published in October 2026. Changes are listed from 27 October 2026 on.

## 27 October 2026: the cost of a student filling an open seat

**What changed.** When students from added homes fill seats in classes that are already running,
the forecast now charges about $7,760 a student a year (FY2027 dollars) instead of $2,497. The new
figure adds special education at the district's average rate and cost to the supplies and other
costs that grow with each seat, because special education staff and services grow with
enrollment even when classrooms don't. [Schools: three tiers for the cost of an added
student](@topic-schools#three-tiers-for-the-cost-of-an-added-student) sets out the calculation.

**What moved.** The base case did not change: it already charges {{fact:student_cost}} for every
added student. Only the cases where added students fill open seats moved:

| Case, effect on the FY2040 budget | Before | Now |
|---|---|---|
| About 2,100 extra homes, seats filled | +$12.9 million | +$8.2 million |
| About 5,100 extra homes, seats filled | +$22.9 million | +$16.7 million |
| About 9,800 extra homes, seats filled | +$31.7 million | +$25.6 million |
| Seats filled at today's pace of building | +$0.9 million | +$0.6 million |
| Growth led by new homes, seats filled | +$24.2 million | +$18.0 million |

A $434,000 rental apartment whose children fill open seats now brings the Town about $1,700 a
year more than it costs, down from about $3,000.

**Also updated.** Open seats now use the School Department's October 1, 2026 counts: 4,436
students in kindergarten through grade 8, about 1,100 open seats under the class-size guidelines
and about 440 with every class kept three students below them. These are text updates; the
forecast's figures did not change.
