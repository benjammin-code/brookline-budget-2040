# Teachers' salary schedules, BEU Unit A, FY2024-FY2027

Source: Brookline School Committee / Brookline Educators Union Unit A contract,
2023-2026 (data/psb_budget/BEU_UnitA_CBA_2023-2026.pdf), Appendix A.1, pages
61-64 of the PDF (contract pages 56-59). The PDF is a scan with no text layer.

Transcription (2026-10-05): pages rendered at 200 dpi, read with macOS Vision
text recognition keeping each number's position, and rebuilt into the step x
lane grid from those positions. One misread corrected by hand (FY2027, step 10,
Bachelor's: read 21,679, is 91,679; the plain OCR pass and the raise check both
give 91,679). Check: every cell equals the prior year's cell times the stated
raise (2.75%, 2.75%, 1.00%) within rounding; 0 failures. FY2024's 2.5% cannot be
checked (the FY2023 schedule is not in this contract).

Columns: step_increase_within_schedule = this step / the step below, same year
(what a step is worth); step_plus_raise_from_prior_year = this step this year /
the step below last year (what a teacher moving up one step receives).

Findings (FY2026 schedule): steps are about equal in dollars ($3,200-3,400), so
they shrink in percent from about 4.5-5.1% at step 2 to 2.4-3.0% at the top;
average 3.6% across lanes. A teacher moving up a step received on average 6.5%
in FY2025 and FY2026 (range 5.2-8.0%) and 4.65% in FY2027; a teacher at the top
step received the raise only. Not included: longevity, stipends, lane changes
(moving to a higher degree column) and any other pay outside the schedule.
