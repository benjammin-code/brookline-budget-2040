## Pay growth per position

Steps, lanes and principal adjustments net of turnover savings add 0.55% to 1.35% (PSB's FY2026 build; the model's 3.7% uses the middle, 0.95%).

- Middle case: 2.54% raise + 0.95% = **3.49%** a year, vs. the model's 3.70%.
- Full range: 2.94% to 4.02%.
- The same arithmetic at the model's 2.75% raise gives 3.70%, so the offer runs -0.21 points against the model in the middle case.

Pay weighting of the top step: top masters step $116,795 vs. $91,602 average on the lower steps (FY2027 grid).

In dollars: on a school payroll of about $134M in FY2028, the middle case is about $0.3M a year below the model in FY2028 and about $0.6M below by FY2029.

Cross-check that does not reconcile: on June 16 the district put the para $2/hr raises at about $1.1M

If the district's figure is right, paras weigh less and the offer runs a little further below 3.7%.

FY2027 (contract year 1) is outside this comparison: the offer's +1.5% Sept 1 and half a year of the
+0.5% mid-year add about 1.75% ($2.3M) on top of the old contract's 1%. The adopted
FY2027 budget doesn't state what raise it reserved; PSB's estimate of +3.78% for Professional Staff
wages in FY2027 (ERSC footnote 28) leaves room for roughly 1-1.5 points beyond the 1% and steps (inference).

Not in this arithmetic: the 6/9/12-week paid parental leave and any change in who is on steps.
