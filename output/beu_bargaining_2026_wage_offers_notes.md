## School Committee (district) offer, Unit A, presented Sept. 17, 2026

Still standing Oct. 27: the district offered a package of "the wage proposals as last presented"
plus parental leave.

Each year (2026-27, 2027-28, 2028-29):
- Sept. 1: +1.5% to all cells except top step, which gets +2.25%. The transcript first says 2.5%;
  the district's negotiator corrected it to "two and a quarter" in the same session.
- Mid-year (around day 91): +0.5% to all cells except top step, which gets +0.25%. The year-1 top-step
  mid-year figure is transcribed as "40%"; read as 0.25% because years 2-3 and Unit B say a quarter percent.
- Normal one-step advance; stipends +2%; double step removed; longevity grievance withdrawn.
- Unit B (Sept. 23): same formula. Year-3 top step is transcribed as "2.4%" and read as 2.25%.
- These come on top of the old contract's 1% that took effect 8/31/2026.

Run-rate schedule increase: about 2.0% a year (non-top cells) and 2.5% (top step). Cost within the
first fiscal year is lower (about 1.75% and 2.4%) because the mid-year raise covers only part of it.

## BEU (union) position

- June presentation, Unit A: raises of 2.0%, 2.5% and 3.0% (+2% more on top step in yr 3), two
  step advances a year, steps cut from 17 to about 12; 3.5% on other money items. Unit B: 3.5%, 4%, 5%.
  Paras: +$5, +$6, +$7/hr and fewer steps.
- Aug. 31 revised Unit A response: removes the steps in year 2 using a "crosswalk"; 17 steps → 12.
  A BEU member said the cost is "less than our original proposal ... 14.7%" over the three years.
  The transcript doesn't say whether that includes step costs. The district asked for a salary grid
  in dollars; none was shown on the recording.
- Sept. 17 Unit B counter: 3%, 4%, and an unclear year-3 figure (transcript garbled).

## Data points said at the table

- School Committee member, Sept. 17: about 60% of teachers are on steps and 40% at top step; a step
  is worth about 3.5%. These are spoken figures, not documented ones.
- Aug. 31: the average step is worth about $3,400 (matches the contract schedules, ~$3,200-3,400).
- The two sides were using different staff placement counts ("scattergrams") to cost proposals.
  The district agreed on Sept. 23 to share its count.

## Forecasting read (reasoning, not data)

At PSB's own FY2026 level-service build (steps 1.59% + lanes 0.14% + principals 0.05%
− breakage 0.42% ≈ 1.36% on top of the raise), the district's offer (raises of about 2.0-2.5% a year)
gives school pay growth of about 3.4-3.8% a year. That matches the model's current 3.7%.
The union's 14.7% over three years is about 4.7% a year if it covers all costs.

Parental leave (district accepts 6/9/12 weeks employer-paid over the three years, with year 1 starting
Jan. 1, 2027) is a new cost that isn't in the model and hasn't been priced.
